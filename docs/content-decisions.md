# Phase 3 content decisions

## Positioning and selection

Quantum Digital offers product engineering and applied AI for founders and product teams. The copy connects web/mobile development, business workflows, integrations, and deployment rather than presenting a list of frameworks. The 15+ years of experience claim comes from the supplied resume.

The homepage’s horizontal row begins with Care Coordination, Accounting with AI Assistance, Research to Action, and Finance Document Assistant, followed by Service Operations Copilot and Robotics Lab. All six retain individual case-study routes; About links to currently published examples from its working practices and current explorations. The owner requested a larger selection presented as a single row rather than a longer vertical catalogue.

## Claims and context

Case studies explain context, challenge, approach, delivery, and engineering focus. The final section describes demonstrated capabilities rather than invented metrics, testimonials, commercial adoption, or measured outcomes. A described feature does not establish medical efficacy, accounting compliance, or production status.

Five common questions cover starting from an idea, existing products, useful AI applications, collaboration with teams, and handover/ongoing work. They make no promises about pricing, start dates, or fixed delivery timelines. Native disclosure controls keep the answers usable without JavaScript. Gold chevrons have a 20px gap before the question text; Motion animates answer height, opacity, and position on opening and closing and respects reduced motion.

The assistant receives the same published case studies, positioning, services, engagements, experience summaries, and questions. It may link to website case studies and only the external project URLs explicitly supplied in that content. Private source files are not passed to the assistant.

## Assistant knowledge and contact direction

The owner explicitly approved the public résumé and portfolio as assistant context. Versioned snapshots in `src/content/resume.md` and `src/content/portfolio.md` include full career history, skills, education, and all seven portfolio projects. Update those snapshots when their parent-folder sources change. They inform answers without automatically adding more homepage cards. Preserve team/lead/independent contributions and distinguish Python-stack adaptability from proven hands-on production experience. Client projects remain abstract without external links. Curated context and targeted published lookup are enough for this corpus; vector RAG is deferred.

Chat persists one anonymous conversation per browser in Convex, rather than adding accounts or a recent-conversations interface. A chat tool may open a contact form and prefill visitor-supplied details. Contact Rudolfs is offered in the chat header; it leads to explicit inquiry review/confirmation and email follow-up, not a live human handoff. The earlier no-contact-form decision now means no standalone form elsewhere on the public website.

The About page pairs this résumé-grounded narrative with an original conceptual Blender workbench, concrete working-practice examples, approved GitHub/LinkedIn profiles, and chat actions. It uses published records for project titles and links; unpublished examples are omitted. The same code-managed journey informs the assistant. Portfolio exploration is distinguished from client delivery, and the Robotics Lab snapshot now includes the implemented warehouse application alongside the simulator, drone planner, and SLAM explorer.

All selected-work pages preserve the saved Context, Challenge, Approach, Delivery, and Engineering focus fields in a “Behind the product.” section. Each field uses the homepage FAQ treatment and a separate expandable row. This keeps the original project detail readable without duplicating it across the main visual story. Abstract-project link restrictions also apply when restoring historical assistant tool results.

## Dedicated service offers

The four mapped service pages explain distinct capabilities: product delivery, AI interpretation inside applications, process coordination, and system-to-system exchange. Current published card fields remain authoritative for identity, introduction, and illustration choice; richer scope, process, FAQs, and related-work candidates are code-managed. Routes, homepage links, sitemap entries, structured page data, and assistant references resolve the same published IDs. Removed services and examples do not return through defaults.

Related work shows engineering capabilities without claiming commercial outcomes or support for an unspecified external vendor. Care and accounting retain their abstract presentation. Research is identified as a private personal project; all three retain their external-link exclusions. Illustrations are labelled conceptual and are independent of shipped product evidence. Structured Person/Service/WebPage data describes the visible offer and approved public profiles; no prices, ratings, reviews, or guaranteed results are added. Full service narratives remain public HTML, while the assistant receives the same curated facts without private planning notes or vector RAG.
