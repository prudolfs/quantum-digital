# Phase 3 content decisions

## Positioning and selection

Quantum Digital offers product engineering and applied AI for founders and product teams. The copy connects web/mobile development, business workflows, integrations, and deployment rather than presenting a list of frameworks. The 15+ years of experience claim comes from the supplied resume.

The homepage’s horizontal row begins with Care Coordination, Accounting with AI Assistance, Research to Action, and Finance Document Assistant, followed by Service Operations Copilot and Robotics Lab. All six retain individual case-study routes; the two additional portfolio examples are also linked from About. The owner requested a larger selection presented as a single row rather than a longer vertical catalogue.

## Claims and context

Case studies explain context, challenge, approach, delivery, and engineering focus. The final section describes demonstrated capabilities rather than invented metrics, testimonials, commercial adoption, or measured outcomes. A described feature does not establish medical efficacy, accounting compliance, or production status.

Five common questions cover starting from an idea, existing products, useful AI applications, collaboration with teams, and handover/ongoing work. They make no promises about pricing, start dates, or fixed delivery timelines. Native disclosure controls keep the answers usable without JavaScript. Gold chevrons have a 20px gap before the question text; Motion animates answer height, opacity, and position on opening and closing and respects reduced motion.

The assistant receives the same published case studies, positioning, services, engagements, experience summaries, and questions. It may link to website case studies and only the external project URLs explicitly supplied in that content. Private source files are not passed to the assistant.

## Assistant knowledge and contact direction

The owner explicitly approved the public résumé and portfolio as assistant context. Versioned snapshots in `src/content/resume.md` and `src/content/portfolio.md` include full career history, skills, education, and all seven portfolio projects. Update those snapshots when their parent-folder sources change. They inform answers without automatically adding more homepage cards. Preserve team/lead/independent contributions and distinguish Python-stack adaptability from proven hands-on production experience. Client projects remain abstract without external links. Curated context and targeted published lookup are enough for this corpus; vector RAG is deferred.

Chat persists one anonymous conversation per browser in Convex, rather than adding accounts or a recent-conversations interface. A chat tool may open a contact form and prefill visitor-supplied details. Contact Rudolfs is offered in the chat header; it leads to explicit inquiry review/confirmation and email follow-up, not a live human handoff. The earlier no-contact-form decision now means no standalone form elsewhere on the public website.
